For positive mass, the positive relativistic kinetic energy obtained after
removing rest energy is exactly the Schrödinger kinetic term plus an explicit
negative correction.  This submission also bounds the correction's magnitude
by a quartic expression in momentum.  The formalization assumes the positive
relativistic mass shell; it does not derive that mass shell, an
arbitrary-wavefunction position-space Schrödinger equation, the Born rule, a
physical particle, or a complete quantum theory.
