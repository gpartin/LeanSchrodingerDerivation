# SCHRODINGER-008: strict certificate

This stage assembles the preceding handoffs into one
`FreeSchrodingerCertificate` for the scoped free nonrelativistic limit.

The certificate records, in one Lean object:

- native mass identification;
- positive mass and nonnegative energy;
- the explicit mass-shell handoff and its positive branch;
- exact rest-energy removal;
- the exact quadratic-plus-remainder expansion;
- the quartic remainder bound;
- the leading free Fourier symbol.

The mass-shell relation remains an explicit input to the certificate. This
stage therefore does not introduce a new spinor construction, hide a target
equation as an assumption, or close the broader quantum-mechanics program.
