# SCHRODINGER-003: positive energy

This stage selects the nonnegative energy branch of the mass-shell relation
and proves that it is the principal square root of the mass-shell expression.

The certificate proves:

- nonnegative energy satisfying `energy^2 = mass^2 + momentum^2` equals
  `relativisticEnergy mass momentum`;
- the relativistic energy is at least the nonnegative mass;
- the packaged proposition `SchrodingerStage03`.

This is the branch-selection handoff needed before removing the rest energy
and taking the nonrelativistic limit.
