# Claim boundary

SCHRODINGER-001 is a formal root-action/mass handoff. SCHRODINGER-002 is a
conditional rewrite of a named free-Dirac mass-shell hypothesis.
SCHRODINGER-003 selects the nonnegative square-root branch under explicit
mass-shell assumptions. SCHRODINGER-004 exactly removes the rest-energy
offset. SCHRODINGER-005 proves the exact rationalized kinetic identity.
SCHRODINGER-006 separates the quadratic kinetic term from an explicit
quartic remainder and proves a positive-mass bound on that remainder.
SCHRODINGER-007 identifies the free Fourier-space symbol while retaining the
exact residual. SCHRODINGER-008 packages the scoped free-limit certificate
with the mass-shell relation still explicit. SCHRODINGER-009 derives the
potential as a local-versus-reference native-mass difference. SCHRODINGER-010
proves the exact shifted-energy split, and SCHRODINGER-011 proves the exact
local relativistic split. Together they do not claim that the public
repository already contains the full Schrodinger derivation, the Born rule, a
complete quantum theory, a complete electron construction, or a physical
measurement model. SCHRODINGER-012 assembles the full potential-bearing
Fourier-energy identity, but the correction remains explicit.

Stage 2 does not independently prove the mass-shell hypothesis from c40
dynamics; it makes the prior result an explicit input and verifies the mass
term handoff in Lean. Stage 3 is an algebraic branch-selection result; it does
not by itself establish the physical state assumptions for every c40 solution.
Stage 4 is exact algebraic re-centering and does not yet establish the
low-momentum approximation. Stage 5 is also exact and keeps the later
relativistic correction visible. Stage 6 proves the correction bound but does
not authorize dropping the correction at arbitrary momentum. Stage 7 is a
Fourier-symbol result and does not establish a general position-space wave
equation or a measurement interpretation.
Stage 8 is an integrated conditional certificate; it does not independently
derive the mass-shell input or construct a spinor field.
Stage 9 derives only the potential identity; it does not yet prove the full
potential-bearing energy expansion. Stage 10 proves only the exact shifted
energy split; it does not yet assemble the full potential-bearing symbol.
Stage 11 remains an exact relativistic identity and does not yet replace the
local kinetic term with the quadratic approximation. Stage 12 does not claim
that its explicit correction vanishes or that a general position-space wave
equation follows automatically. Stage 13 adds a rigorously differentiated
plane-wave mode and proves the textbook operator identity for that mode in
natural units, with the exact correction still retained by the Stage 12
certificate. It does not claim the arbitrary-wavefunction functional-analytic
extension, Born's rule, or a complete quantum theory.

The promoted definitions file contains the declarations required by the
thirteen public stages. The public closeout is still a scoped formal
derivation, not an experimental validation or a complete quantum theory.
