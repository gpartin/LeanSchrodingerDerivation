# Claim boundary

SCHRODINGER-001 is a formal root-action/mass handoff, and SCHRODINGER-002 is
a conditional rewrite of a named free-Dirac mass-shell hypothesis. Together
they are not a claim that the public repository already contains the full
Schrodinger derivation, the Born rule, a complete quantum theory, a complete
electron construction, or a physical measurement model.

Stage 2 does not independently prove the mass-shell hypothesis from c40
dynamics; it makes the prior result an explicit input and verifies the mass
term handoff in Lean.

The promoted definitions file contains only the declarations required by the
two public stages, and no later-stage proof modules are included.
